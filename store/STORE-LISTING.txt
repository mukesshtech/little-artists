Little Artists is a safe, ad-free creativity and learning app made just for kids! 🎨

🖍️ COLORING BOOK
• 14 fun pictures — cat, fish, car, rocket, butterfly, apple, ice cream and more
• Magic one-tap colour fill — just tap an area to fill it
• Brushes, stickers and an eraser too

✏️ FREE DRAW & PAINT
• A blank canvas for big imaginations
• Crayon, marker, glowing neon and rainbow brushes
• 16 bright colours, 3 brush sizes, fun stickers

🔤 FUN LEARNING (with sound!)

• 123 — numbers and counting
• Shapes and Animals (hear the animal sounds!)
• Friendly talking voice helps kids learn by listening

🖼️ MY GALLERY
• Save artwork with one tap
• View, delete and share creations with family

WHY PARENTS LOVE IT
✓ 100% kid-safe — NO ads, NO pop-ups
✓ NO internet needed — play anywhere
✓ NO sign-up, NO data collection, NO in-app purchases
✓ Simple, colourful design for ages 2–8

Download Little Artists and let the creativity begin! 🌈
