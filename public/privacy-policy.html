🎨 Little Artists — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Little Artists mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. We are committed to protecting the privacy of every child and family who uses the App.

The short version

Little Artists does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The App works completely offline and needs no account, login, or internet connection.

Information we do NOT collect

  • No name, email, phone number, or address.
  • No location data.
  • No photos from your device (the App cannot access your camera or gallery).
  • No usage analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers.
  • No account or sign-in of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Artwork that a child saves ("My Gallery") is stored only on the device using local browser/app storage. It never leaves the device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the App or clearing its data removes these drawings permanently.

Advertising

The App contains no advertisements and no third-party ad networks.

Third-party services

The App does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics. Text-to-speech (for the Learning section) is provided by the device's own operating system and is processed on-device.

Children's privacy

Little Artists is designed for children. Because we collect no personal data at all, the App is compliant with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K). We never knowingly collect data from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
